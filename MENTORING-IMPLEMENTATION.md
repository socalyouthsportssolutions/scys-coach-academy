# Coach Mentoring — combined implementation and review

10 November 2026. Local development only; no installation or publication authorised.

The 9 November programme supplies the coaching content and examples. The accompanying Codex prompt adds implementable acceptance criteria. Neither document is evidence of delivered functionality. Where they differ, this implementation uses account-linked Hub records rather than synchronising the internal request sheet, and keeps mentoring separate from foundations and lesson reflections.

Plan: seven reusable lessons in one category, an optional shared course/pathway, printable blank field cards, private request/visit records, account ownership and current-assignment checks, optimistic concurrency and retry protection, then build and isolated verification.

Real pilot collection remains blocked until coaching-lead approval, technical/privacy acceptance, an approved reporting procedure, retention/deletion/backup rules and coordinator capacity are agreed. No retention period is invented.
