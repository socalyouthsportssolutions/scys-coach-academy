Responsive

The same shell at phone and tablet widths, each in its own frame so the real breakpoints apply (≤ 700 px: horizontal nav row with a thin teal-700 scrollbar, single column; ≤ 1000 px: narrower sidebar, stacked dashboard).

Focus, reduced motion and forced colours

Tab through the app shell above. Focus is a 3 px ink outline plus a 6 px yellow halo: the ink ring carries contrast on white, the yellow halo on the dark header and the current nav item. Keep both halves.

With “reduce motion” switched on in the operating system, transitions and the library-card lift are removed. In Windows high-contrast (forced colours) the outline remains, the primary button gets a system border and the current nav item a highlight outline.

Voice

UK English (centre, programme, organisation). Child-centred, warm, direct and plain. Buttons say what happens. Errors say what happened and what to do. Never invite details that identify a child. UK English and the child-centred header line are established choices.
