# Pilot catalogue content notes

Prepared 3 November 2026. This is local pilot learning content, not an independently validated programme or a live learning system. Online completion does not demonstrate on-field competence.

## Scope and provenance

- Six lessons, each 250–450 words excluding questions; three meaningful scenario questions and answer explanations per lesson. Five coach foundations; volunteers share the first two and have one dedicated support lesson. First two preserve approved wording and intent from the accepted dashboard preview.
- Sixteen distinct resource cards, six pathways (U6, U8, U10, U12, U14, volunteer), and three mentor-required practical tasks. Age tags and pathways are proposed navigation and sequencing, not fixed developmental prescriptions.
- All nine supplied guide PDFs are indexed with verified original local paths. Catalogue use is selective: games come from the U8–U14 e-book, Coach Brandon guide and 2026 Coaches Guide. Near-duplicate 11–14 guides are not counted as separate evidence. Indexing is not full critical appraisal.
- References use 1-based PDF page numbers. References identify the foundation of an adaptation, not that every sentence is a quotation. Explanations, scenarios, tasks, pathways and observation cues are proposed learning copy.
- Six original full-page JPEG visuals were rendered from supplied PDF pages at 850 pixels wide. Original page attribution remains visible. In version 0.2.0 they are stored as separate media files/modules and loaded only with the requested lesson or activity. The former inline data URIs totalled 1,153,910 bytes; they are no longer part of the startup catalogue. No full PDF or third-party research paper is embedded.

## Important distinctions

Intent over outcome is a coaching value and a parent-facing way to counter outcome bias. It is not treated as a statistical fact requiring proof. Outcomes remain useful information; purposeful thought and courage also merit recognition.

All support and challenge entries have a visible Proposed option prefix and adaptationsProposed=true. If an option also appears in its source, the card says so. Equipment lists and additional setup decisions inferred for use are labelled proposed. Missing dimensions, player counts and durations are explicitly stated. Individual needs, available facilities and lead-coach judgement must guide use.

The sources sometimes use waiting or exclusion consequences. Cards retain provenance and explicitly identify proposed alternatives where supplied. Safeguarding references defer to current programme procedures; this catalogue is not a full safeguarding policy or risk assessment. Volunteer roles and progression are proposed and require agreement with the programme lead. Reflection prompts exclude names and identifying personal details about children.

No old season calendar, staff roster, unverified dropout percentage, universal touches multiplier, fixed training ratio or retained-learning promise is reproduced in the lesson prose. Guide pages carrying those statistics were not selected as visuals. Detailed methodology remains reserved for separate later work.

## Research boundary

The synthesis and selected evidence-register entries informed this content. The five research entries preserve limitations and identify proposed applications. Original papers are linked without redistribution. No new consequential empirical claim was introduced and original study methods were not newly re-appraised. Older youth and academy findings must not be presented as direct proof for U6–U8. Research does not independently validate the full SCYS approach.

## Validation

Checked JSON parse, lesson word counts (320–346 words before final anonymity edit), three-question structure, zero-based answer indices, pathway resource references, lesson media references, original source file existence, page ranges and embedded-media budget. Source PDFs were preserved. Application rendering and interactive checks remain integration work for the parent agent.

Outputs: hub/source/catalogue.json (lightweight index), hub/source/content/ (individual lessons, activities and visuals), and hub/source/content-notes.md. The original content is unchanged by the modular refactor.
